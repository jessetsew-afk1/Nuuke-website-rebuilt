**Effective date:** December 25, 2025

At NUUKE, we operate with the same precision and security in our data handling as we do in our architecture. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our services via nuuke.us.

## 1. Zero-share communication policy

Your privacy is our priority. **NUUKE does not sell, rent, or share your personal communication data with third parties for marketing or any other purposes.**

- **SMS & mobile privacy:** No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
- **Consent integrity:** All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
- **Email addresses:** Your email is used strictly for mission-critical updates and direct communication.
- **Other communication:** Any other form of relevant communication (Slack, WhatsApp, or direct messaging) is kept strictly between our team and your organization.

## 2. Information we collect

To provide our creative and technical services, we collect the following:

- **Identification data:** Name, business name, and job title.
- **Contact information:** Email address and phone number provided via inquiry forms.
- **Technical data:** IP addresses, browser types, and device identifiers used to access our site.
- **Project briefs:** Any proprietary information shared during the "Mission Brief" or onboarding process.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your data exclusively to:

- Initialize and manage your projects.
- Communicate regarding technical updates or service deliveries.
- Improve the performance of our digital infrastructure.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Data security & retention

- **Encryption:** All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.
- **Retention:** We only keep your information as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with statutory requirements.

## 5. Your rights

Regardless of your geography, NUUKE respects your right to control your data. You may:

- Request access to the data we hold about you.
- Request the immediate deletion of your personal records from our active systems.
- Opt out of any non-essential communication at any time.

## 6. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to external project management tools or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.
