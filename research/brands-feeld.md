# NUUKE concept case study: Feeld brand brief

This is an independent concept, labelled **"Concept, not commissioned by the brand"**. The facts below come from public sources and are cited at the end.

**Method and caveats (read first)**
- Research date: 6 Oct 2026. Web search and page fetches. Facts come from Feeld's own site (homepage, The App, Trust & Safety, Ask Feeld, press releases), the App Store listing, Wikipedia, design press (It's Nice That, The Brand Identity, Fonts In Use) and business press (The Guardian via syndication, Global Dating Insights). Forbes and the Feeld help centre returned 403 to our fetcher, so facts from them are taken from search snippets and are marked. **Re-check numbers and quotes against the primary URL before publishing.** App Store ratings and prices are snapshots.
- **Feeld does not publish hex colours.** Hex values below are *our working approximation* from the public description of the 2023 identity (aura gradients in pink, purple, orange and green on black and white). They are not brand specs.
- Personas, design problems, prototype screens, the cinematic opening and every interactive "lab" are **NUUKE assumptions and ideas**, not brand claims.
- `[unverified]` marks a claim from one weak source, or a point where sources disagree. Skip those on the page.
- Content sensitivity: Feeld serves adults exploring non-monogamy, kink and queer identities. Our case keeps to identity, consent, privacy, safety and inclusion. No explicit desire names, imagery or language are used on the page.

## Company summary
- **What it is:** a dating app "for the curious", for people "open to experiencing people and relationships in new ways" (feeld.co/about). It supports singles, couples and multi-partner relationships, with polyamory, consensual non-monogamy, pansexuality, asexuality and kink named on its About page.
- **Founded:** 2014 in London by Bulgarian-born designer **Dimo Trifonov**. Wikipedia and Trending Topics say he conceived it after his then-girlfriend **Ana Kirova** told him she had feelings for other people. Both were London-based graphic designers.
- **Launch and rename:** launched worldwide as **3nder** in July 2014. Match Group (Tinder's parent) sued 3nder for trademark infringement in May 2016, and the app was renamed **Feeld** in August 2016 (Wikipedia).
- **Leadership:** Trifonov was CEO until March 2021. Kirova joined in 2016 "as an all-rounder with no specific title", became Chief Product Officer in 2020 and CEO in **April 2021** (Wikipedia; the Feeld article lists March 2021 `[month differs between the two Wikipedia pages]`). Kyle Brennan is quoted by The Guardian as Feeld President (Oct 2026).
- **Ownership:** the founders retain majority ownership (Global Dating Insights, citing Companies House).
- **Team:** "80+ team members around the world" (feeld.co/press).
- **Funding:** an early seed round from UK group Haatch, then $500,000 from angel investors in October 2015 (Wikipedia).
- **Business model:** free to join, with the **Majestic** membership and consumables. App Store in-app purchases (US snapshot): Single Ping $2.99, 5 Pings $13.49, 10 Pings $20.99, 20 Pings $34.99, Uplift $9.99, Majestic 30 days $29.99, 90 days $49.99, 1 year $149.99.
- **Seller / category / age:** Feeld Ltd · Lifestyle · 18+ (App Store).

## Brand look (2023 identity)
- **Relaunch:** 14 Nov 2023, ahead of the tenth anniversary, with a full replatforming described as Feeld's largest tech investment to date (Global Dating Insights, It's Nice That).
- **Agency and team:** Made Thought (identity, gradients); Concept Bureau (strategy); **Charlotte Rohde** (custom typeface and wordmark); **Molly Matalon** (photography of 13 community members in New York). In-house: Andrew Peet (VP Creative), Nyomi Warren (Lead Brand Designer) and others (The Brand Identity).
- **Type:** a custom version of Rohde's **New Edge 666**, "FEELD Edge Rounded", variable on two axes (Ultra Light to Ultra Bold, Straight to Rounded), with custom ligatures that suggest connection, "like merging pools of water or ink" (It's Nice That). Supporting face: **ABC Diatype** (Fonts In Use). Rohde: the range from light to ultra bold and the rounding "resembles the idea of the perfect switch."
- **Colour:** an **Aura** system of heat-map-like gradients made from footage of dancers, performers and actors touching and moving; it avoids the rainbow trope and is reserved for emotive moments such as connecting or liking (It's Nice That, The Brand Identity). Palette described as pink, purple, orange, green, black, white, brown and grey.
- **Motion:** "inspired by the human body", with animation modes that mimic **breath, heartbeat and touch** (Global Dating Insights).
- **Our working palette (approximation, not official):** Ink `#0E0B12`, Bone `#F5F0EA`, Lilac `#C7B6FF`, Coral `#FF9E7A`, Rose `#FF6FB5`, Violet `#7B4DFF`, Ember `#FF5A3C`, Mint `#9FE3C0`.
- **Tone:** warm, frank, inclusive, playful ("Nothing wild to report here" in release notes). We keep our copy clean and literal.

## Product features (as publicly described)
- **Identity:** 20+ genders and 20+ sexualities, "and change them as many times as you like"; an interactive glossary of gender, sexuality and desire terms (feeld.co/the-app).
- **Desires:** 50+ to choose from; Majestic members can filter by Desires.
- **Bios:** a public bio and a **hidden bio**, which is concealed in Discover and shown only to members you've liked or Pinged, in their Likes and Connections tabs (Feeld help centre, via search snippet).
- **Private photos:** "profile images that are only visible to your connections" (Ask Feeld).
- **Likes and Connections:** Likes are subtle and only show if interest is mutual; a mutual like makes a Connection (feeld.co Pings page).
- **Pings:** a bolder hello with a personal note; your profile "jumps to the top of their New Connections queue". Majestic members get one Ping every 24 hours. Members can **disable Ping notes** (Ask Feeld).
- **Uplift:** a 24-hour visibility boost in Discover; others don't know you used it.
- **Constellation (linked profiles):** link with up to 5 people (partners, friends and more), with 20+ relationship descriptors; invitation links last 72 hours; free for all members (press release, help centre snippet).
- **Group chats:** "Chat, flirt and build up the anticipation for IRL meets using group chats." (feeld.co)
- **Incognito (Majestic):** "you can view profiles in Discover without showing up in other people's Discover, unless you like them first" (Ask Feeld).
- **Screenshot protection:** images sent in chat cannot be screenshot (feeld.co).
- **Nudity detection:** opt-in; explicit images in chat are blurred automatically, with no human review (feeld.co, help centre snippet).
- **Verification:** "biometric identity verification" / photo verification using facial recognition (feeld.co, Trust & Safety).
- **Safety team:** a Global Safety Team available 24/7; report from the options menu (Trust & Safety).
- **No ads; no profile ranking.**
- Not found publicly, so **not claimed**: exact distance display rules, location fuzzing. Our Privacy Lab marks "approximate distance" as a NUUKE concept idea.

## Notable history (context only, not used on the page)
- Late 2023: outage and reported privacy concerns during the relaunch (Wikipedia).
- March 2024: security firm Fortbridge reported API vulnerabilities; Feeld said fixes were deployed by 28 May 2024 and that it had no evidence data was accessed (Feeld help centre snippet, Fortbridge). This is why privacy has to be visible and verifiable in the UI.

## Audience and personas (our assumptions)
1. **"Newly curious" Maya, 43**, recently out of a long marriage, exploring what she wants (women over 40 are a fast-growing segment).
2. **"Opening up" Jo and Theo, 30s**, nesting partners who want to be transparent from the first screen (Constellation, group chats).
3. **"Privacy-first" Rae, 27, they/them**, who wants to be seen by the right people and invisible to everyone else (State of Dating Vol. II safety findings).

## Design problems (our framing)
1. **Identity without dropdowns.** 20+ genders, 20+ sexualities and 50+ desires, chosen without forced binaries, explained in plain words, and easy to change (90% of members update their bios).
2. **Consent as an interaction.** Layers of a profile (hidden bio, private photos) should open only when both people choose to, and that rule must be legible.
3. **Profiles for more than one person.** Linked profiles need consent from every person, clear labels and a fair way to say hello to a constellation.
4. **Privacy you can feel working.** Incognito, private photos, blur and screenshot protection should show their effect, not hide in settings.
5. **Safety without fear.** Reporting and blocking should be one tap away and calm in tone.

## Cinematic opening (our idea)
A scroll-scrubbed heat-map "aura" field: two cold, boxy options (a binary dropdown) melt into a spectrum; two auras drift in from either side, each with a hidden core; one says yes, then the other, and only then do they merge and reveal. Lockup: "Both say yes." A marquee rolls through identity and relationship words.
